Connect cards

Ready accounts are registered, waiver-verified, and have no active card. Newest registrations appear first.

Connect one card at a time

  1. Select the person.
    Confirm the kiosk shows their name.
  2. Have them tap and wait.
    Keep the card at the blue hand until the screen changes.
  3. Check the result.
    Move on only after the kiosk says CARD CONNECTED.

If their name stays on this list, the card was not saved. Select them again and have them re-tap.
